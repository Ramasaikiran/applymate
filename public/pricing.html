ApplyMate pricing

Last updated: October 23, 2026

ApplyMate has one free plan and three paid plans. Paid plans last 30 days, are a one-time payment, and do not auto-renew. Prices depend on the country you choose at signup.

How pricing works outside India

India pays in Indian rupees. Every other supported country pays in its own currency, using the same number as the US dollar price. For example, Pro is $50 in the United States, £50 in the United Kingdom and €50 in Germany, France, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain and Italy.

Supported countries: India, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Germany, France, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain and Italy. The country you pick at signup sets your currency and cannot be changed afterwards.

Billing terms

  • Payment is collected upfront through Razorpay. ApplyMate never stores card or bank details.
  • Each paid plan runs 30 days from payment. There is no auto-renewal and no lock-in.
  • The 7-day trial is available once per account on Basic, Pro and Max Pro.
  • You can cancel any time from your dashboard. Access continues until the period ends.
  • Refunds follow the refund and cancellation policy: unused days are refundable minus days used and Razorpay's 2% fee, with exceptions described there.

Which plan fits

  • Free or Basic if you want to choose and submit applications yourself and only need good matches surfaced daily.
  • Pro if you want the team to do the applying, with a tailored cover letter for each job.
  • Max Pro if you also want interview support.

ApplyMate does not guarantee interviews or job offers. See the FAQ for what the service does and does not do.
