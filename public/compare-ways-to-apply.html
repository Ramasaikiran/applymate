When applying yourself is the better choice

  • You are targeting a short list of companies and want to personalise every application.
  • You have referrals or contacts at the companies you want.
  • You enjoy the process and have the time.

ApplyMate's Free and Basic plans fit this approach: they surface a daily matched job feed and you do the applying.

When delegating makes sense

  • You are employed and cannot spend hours a day on applications.
  • You are a fresher applying widely and want each application to carry a tailored cover letter.
  • You want a log of every application and every skipped job.

What none of them can promise

No method guarantees interviews or offers, and ApplyMate does not either. Daily volume depends on how many suitable jobs are open.
