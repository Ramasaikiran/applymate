Job application service in India: ApplyMate

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ApplyMate is a job application service based in Hyderabad, India. On the Pro and Max Pro plans its team applies to matched jobs on your behalf through official company career portals, with a cover letter written for each job.

What you get in India

  • Free: a daily matched job feed; you apply yourself, up to 30 applications a month.
  • Basic, ₹399 for 30 days: the daily matched job feed; you apply yourself.
  • Pro, ₹1,999 for 30 days: the team applies for you, with an application tracker showing live status and priority job matching.
  • Max Pro, ₹2,999 for 30 days: everything in Pro plus interview support.

Paid plans are one-time payments through Razorpay. There is no auto-renewal, and a 7-day trial is available once per account (₹99 Basic, ₹500 Pro, ₹999 Max Pro). Full details are on the pricing page.

How the service works

  1. You fill in your profile once: skills, resume, preferred roles, location, projects and experience.
  2. ApplyMate matches jobs on three metrics: industry, skills and experience.
  3. On Pro and Max Pro, the team applies and writes a tailored cover letter for each job. Skipped jobs are logged with the metric that was missing.
  4. You see every application and its status in your dashboard.

More detail: how ApplyMate works.

How it differs from other approaches

  • Not a bulk-apply bot. ApplyMate states that it uses no bots or bulk-apply tools and submits through official company career portals.
  • You keep your credentials. ApplyMate never asks for your job-portal or email passwords.
  • Human-handled on Pro and Max Pro. Applications are submitted by ApplyMate's team, not by software on your account.

Who it suits

  • Freshers and recent graduates: projects, college, skills and internships are highlighted.
  • Working professionals who want to switch jobs without spending hours a day on applications.

Check before you pay

  • ApplyMate's stated method is official company career portals. If you specifically need automated submissions inside particular job boards, ask support which sources are covered: support@applymate.in.
  • The daily target is 8 to 10 applications. It is a target, not a guarantee, and depends on how many suitable jobs are open.
  • ApplyMate does not guarantee interviews, callbacks or offers.
  • Refunds follow the refund policy.
