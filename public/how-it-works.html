How ApplyMate works

Last updated: October 23, 2026

ApplyMate matches your profile to open jobs and, on the Pro and Max Pro plans, applies for you through official company career portals. Every screened job is logged in your dashboard.

The three steps

  1. Fill your profile (about 4 minutes, one time). Add your skills, resume, preferred roles, location, projects, college and experience.
  2. Pick a plan (about 2 minutes). Start free, or choose Basic, Pro or Max Pro. See pricing.
  3. ApplyMate applies daily. On Pro and Max Pro the team finds matches and applies with a cover letter written for each specific job. You are notified of every application.

How a job is matched

ApplyMate applies only when a job matches you on three metrics: industry, skills and experience. If a job is skipped, your dashboard shows which metric was missing, so you can see why.

How applications are submitted

  • Applications go through official company career portals only.
  • No bots and no bulk-apply tools.
  • ApplyMate never asks for your job-portal or email passwords.
  • Each application has a cover letter tailored to the job description, using your relevant skills and projects.

What you can see in your dashboard

  • Every application: company, role, date, matched skills and status.
  • Every skipped job and the metric that was missing.

How many applications per day

The target is 8 to 10 tailored applications a day once your profile is active. It is a target, not a guarantee. The real number depends on how many suitable open jobs match your profile, and it can be lower on weekends, holidays or when few good roles are open. ApplyMate does not apply to unsuitable jobs just to reach a number.

Who it is for

  • College students and recent graduates looking for a first job. ApplyMate highlights projects, college, skills and internships.
  • Working professionals who want to switch jobs without spending hours a day on applications.

Limits to know about

  • ApplyMate does not guarantee interviews, callbacks or offers.
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Your profile and resume need to be accurate and kept up to date for good matching.
