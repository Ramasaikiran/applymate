The Free plan costs nothing in every country: a daily matched job feed, you apply yourself, up to 30 applications a month.

Rules that apply everywhere

  • You choose your country at signup. It sets your currency and cannot be changed afterwards.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay and are one-time for 30 days, with no auto-renewal.
  • The 7-day trial is available once per account.
  • Refunds are calculated in your own currency under the refund policy.
  • The prices shown at checkout are the ones charged. Prices may change, and existing subscribers are notified before any increase.

What each plan includes

  • Basic: daily matched job feed; you apply yourself.
  • Pro: ApplyMate's team applies for you through official career portals, with an application tracker and priority job matching.
  • Max Pro: everything in Pro plus interview support.
